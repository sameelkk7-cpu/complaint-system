About the Residents' Complaint Desk

What is this site?

The Residents' Complaint Desk is a private, internally operated web portal designed for the residents and committee members of a residential housing society. Its sole purpose is to allow residents to formally report noise and nuisance complaints (e.g. dog barking, loud music, construction noise) within the community, and for the elected committee to track, review, and resolve those complaints.

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government authority, bank, telecommunications provider, or any third-party commercial service. It is a stand-alone, self-hosted application for internal community use only.

Who operates this site?

This portal is operated by the residential society's management committee. Access is strictly limited to:

  • Residents — individuals who have registered and been approved by the admin
  • Committee members — elected residency committee staff responsible for handling complaints
  • Admin — the society's designated system administrator

New registrations require manual approval by the admin before sign-in is possible. Unapproved or rejected accounts cannot log in.

How are credentials handled?

Your email address and password are transmitted only to this society's own backend server (complaint-system-backend-gtrc.onrender.com) over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Passwords are stored as irreversible cryptographic hashes (bcrypt) — plain-text passwords are never retained. No credentials are shared with, forwarded to, or accessible by any third party.

What data is collected?

When you submit a complaint, the following information may be stored:

  • Your name, flat/unit number, and email (provided at registration)
  • Complaint description, category, and reported incident time
  • Optional photo or audio evidence you attach to support the complaint
  • An AI-generated audio classification result (produced on-server using a local YAMNet model — audio files are never sent to an external AI service)

All data is stored within the society's own database. It is used solely for managing complaints and is not sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties.

AI audio verification

When you attach an audio recording to a complaint, an on-premises AI model (YAMNet, Apache-2.0, by Google) classifies the sound (e.g. "Dog Barking", "Loud Music"). This classification helps the committee prioritise complaints. The model runs entirely on this society's own infrastructure; your audio is never uploaded to an external AI provider.

If the AI classification is unavailable (e.g. the service is starting up), the complaint is flagged for manual review by the committee — it is never silently discarded.

Contact

If you have questions about this portal or your account, please contact your society's committee via the usual community communication channels.
